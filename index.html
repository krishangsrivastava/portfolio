Engineering Mindset

OBSERVE. ANALYSE. OPTIMISE.

I'm Krishang Srivastava, a Mechanical Engineering student at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai, passionate about motorsport, vehicle dynamics and understanding the science behind vehicle performance.

My journey in motorsport began with Formula Student at Camber Racing, where I've progressed from a Vehicle Dynamics Trainee to Brakes and Vehicle Testing Lead. This experience has taught me the importance of engineering fundamentals, teamwork, precision and learning through practical experience.

I'm particularly interested in understanding how a car behaves on track — how tyres generate forces, how suspension influences handling, how weight transfer affects vehicle balance, and how engineering decisions translate into real-world performance.

Observe. Analyse. Optimise. These principles guide how I approach both engineering and everyday life. I believe in observing before making assumptions, analysing evidence before reaching conclusions, and continuously improving through experience and reflection.

My goal is to build a career in motorsport and vehicle performance engineering, where curiosity, analytical thinking and a willingness to learn can contribute to meaningful improvements on track.
